What varies

The image grid, so rows are g² and the ten columns stay the ten classes. The window, the drive, the cell pitch and the differential pair are held fixed, so a wire segment is the same resistance at every size.

What is recorded, and what is live

A verdict comes from the recorded sweep over all 2,000 digits. The numbers that move as you drag are computed here, over a 500-digit sample of that set — the same 500 at every size, and the same 500 MATLAB recorded a twin for.

Which grid is the shared task

Only 6×6. The others are the same digits at another resolution and are internal to this repository; nothing here is compared to a photonn row.
